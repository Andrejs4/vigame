# Pictures

The pictures here are icons from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net)
(source: [github.com/game-icons/icons](https://github.com/game-icons/icons)),
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
licence. They are changed only in form: the black background square is
removed and the icon left unfilled, for the page to colour. The meeples
(`unit.svg`, and the four in `band.svg`) are also made narrower and a
little taller, their heads less narrowed than their bodies, to look more
like people: the author's idea. The band's four stand closer together.

Replacing one: keep the file name, a square `viewBox`, `width` and `height`
attributes (Firefox draws an SVG on a canvas only with them), one colour
(the page recolours it), and no scripts or outside references. Update the
table.

## Pictures generated with AI

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `favicon.png` | the browser tab's icon (32 × 32): a band of heroes |
| `banner.webp` | the login page's banner above the form (1040 × 400): a crowd of townsfolk and soldiers at dusk |
| `banner-lord.webp` | the login page's banner below the form (1040 × 400): the Dark Lord's castle, and ogres and ghouls coming at the viewer |

These were generated with Perplexity's image generation, from prompts by
Vigame's author, Andrejs Petrovs (Andrejs4 on GitHub), on an individual
plan. Perplexity allows images made on those plans for personal,
non-commercial use only. So they are not covered by Vigame's licences: they
are here for this non-commercial game alone, and anyone reusing Vigame,
above all for anything commercial, should leave them out or replace them.

## The author's own pictures

Pictures made for Vigame by its author, Andrejs Petrovs (Andrejs4 on
GitHub), drawn by hand or generated with Stable Diffusion run on his own
machine, are under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
licence (CC BY-SA 4.0): anyone may use and change them, crediting the author,
and share what they make of them under the same terms.

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `portraits-men.png` | 64 portraits of men, 32 × 32 each, in an 8 × 8 sheet |
| `portraits-women.png` | 64 portraits of women, 32 × 32 each, in an 8 × 8 sheet |
| `portraits.hex` | the portraits' 14 colours, one a line: faces added later use these to match |
| `portrait.svg` | a head-and-shoulders silhouette, the portrait of a unit that isn't a hero (one colour, made for Vigame) |
| `ground-grass-1.webp`, `ground-grass-2.webp` | grass from above, two versions, a hex each (222 × 256) |
| `ground-meadow-1.webp` | meadow from above, a hex |
| `ground-scrub-1.webp` | scrub from above: trees and bushes on bare ground, a hex |
| `ground-water-1.webp`, `ground-water-2.webp` | water from above, a lighter and a darker version, a hex each |

The portraits were generated with Stable Diffusion (1.5 and SDXL base, whose
licences allow using their images), then framed around each face, put on one
grey background, shrunk to 32 × 32 and given one shared palette with the
`web-pixel-images` skill from
[Andrejs4/my-claude-skills](https://github.com/Andrejs4/my-claude-skills).
The full-size originals are not in this repository.

The ground's pictures were generated with Stable Diffusion too, from above,
then recoloured to each terrain's colour on the board, shrunk and cut to a
hex by `scripts/ground.py`. Their originals aren't in this repository
either.
