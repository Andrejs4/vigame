        How to play The units at home in your castle raise new ones: the more of them, and the better they breed, the sooner. Build near your standing buildings: the crew you pick raises it once they get there, and meanwhile it has half its hit points and shelters nobody. Select a building and press Crew to choose who works there; Return sends them home. A pit's crew digs stone, which pays for towers, farms and upgrades; farms grow food. Every minute your units eat; short meals make them hungry, and at full hunger they may starve. Select a wagon or band, then click a cell, to move it; it carries its crew. A wagon can't pass other buildings; a band passes anything of yours, gives no cover, and breaks up when it has nobody. Units in buildings and bands strike enemies in reach; press Attack, then an enemy building, to choose their target. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom; press the minimap to look anywhere. A button's bold letter is its key.
