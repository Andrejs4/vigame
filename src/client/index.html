            How to play

            The units at home in your castle raise new ones: the more of them, and the better they breed, the sooner. Your side is out once your castle falls.

            Build near your standing buildings. The crew you pick raises a building once they get there; meanwhile it has half its hit points and shelters nobody. A pit's crew digs stone, which pays for towers, farms and upgrades; farms grow food. Water can't be crossed, and scrub can't be built on and is twice as slow to walk.

            Select a building and press Crew to choose who works there; Return sends them home. Your castle's Heroes lists your heroes, who level up faster than the rest. Every minute your units eat: short meals make them hungry, and at full hunger they may starve.

            Select a wagon or band, then click a cell, to move it; it carries its crew. A wagon can't pass other buildings; a band passes anything of yours, gives no cover, and breaks up when it has nobody. Units in buildings and bands strike enemies in reach; press Attack, then an enemy building, to choose their target.

            Drag to pan, wheel to zoom; press the minimap to look anywhere. A button's bold letter is its key; choosing a crew, Enter or that letter again sends it.

            About

            Vigame is a real-time strategy game on a hex map, played in the browser: a prototype, still changing.

            Its source code, and the place to report a bug or suggest something: github.com/Andrejs4/vigame.
